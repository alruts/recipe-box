# Instructions

1. Protect rubber gasket around steel trim (q tip and tape)
1. Take borvél and grind down the bubbles
1. 80 grit sandpaper down to bare metal with sanding block
    - cross pattern sanding
    - get rid of all of the rust
1. finish off with borvél (all rust must go)
1. do thin coat of body filler, less is better than too much, you can always add more
1. 80 grit sand real quick, not much sanding, cover wider area than only the filler
1. 180 paper, slightly larger area
    - remember cross pattern
1. when feels even, you are done if you have holes fill them
1. sand smooth 320 grit 
1. sand smooth 400 grit until all smooth
    - remember bigger area is better, needs to feel smooth
1. clean with cleaner and cloth
1. if surface is perfectly smooth, use self etching primer
1. since we ground to bare metal, this primer is perfect
1. quick sanding with 400 grit paper, to ensure surface is perfectly smooth
    - not too much to go through primer
1. blow any dust out with air
1. clean with cleaner ! needs to be very clean

## painting
1. prep with paper to protect windshield
1. lite coat at first, its ok if it does not fully cover
1. another lite coat
1. and the third coat

let dry
1. wipe clean to make sure no dust (wipe large area)
1. spay clear coat again lite coat (far away more than color coat)
1. other coat, tad bit heavier than first coat
1. third coat is wet coat, should be thick without creating running or sag
1. blend it in to the original finish on the roof, looks glossy now but will blend

voila!

## polish
can polish with orbital buffer, optional

or just clean normal polish
